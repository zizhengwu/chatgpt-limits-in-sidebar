# ChatGPT limits in sidebar

Shows your remaining 5-hour and weekly ChatGPT limits beside your ChatGPT profile.

![ChatGPT limits and reset countdowns in the ChatGPT sidebar](store/screenshot-1280x800.png)

## Chrome extension

1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
3. Reload ChatGPT and expand the sidebar.

## Userscript

With Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey installed, [install the script](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zizhengwu/chatgpt-limits-in-sidebar/main/chatgpt-limits.user.js) and reload ChatGPT.
